Name the look as a result ("warm highlights, lifted blacks, visible grain"), say which layer it goes on, and ask for a before and after. Effects remove detail, so the picture needs detail to begin with.

## Base prompt

```text title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Grade the photo warmer with a little more contrast, then add grain at a low strength. Keep the headline text clean: grade only the photo layer. Show frame 0 before and after.
```

## The knobs that matter

| What to say | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **The look as a result** | "Warm highlights, lifted blacks, visible grain" says what you will see. |
| **Which layer** | Grading the text too is rarely what you want. |
| **How strong** | A strength or a percentage stops it going too far. |
| **A before and after** | You see exactly what the look changed. |

## Common failure modes

**Naming a style only.**
- **Avoid:** "make it cinematic"
- **Instead:** "warm highlights, lifted blacks, a little grain"

**Grading everything.**
- **Avoid:** "add a colour grade"
- **Instead:** "grade only the photo layer"

**No frame check.**
- **Avoid:** "apply the look and render"
- **Instead:** "show frame 0 before and after"

## In more detail

## Say what the look does

Ask for the result and the agent picks the effect:

| Ask for | The agent reaches for |
|---|---|
| Warmer, more contrast, lifted blacks | `colorGrade`, or `colorCorrection`, `levels` and `curves` |
| A film stock or a preset look | `lut` with a `.cube` file you supply |
| Two-tone, thermal, duotone | `gradientMap` |
| Film grain, print dots, a dot-matrix screen | `grain`, `halftone`, `dotMatrix`, `dither` |
| Glow, soft bloom | `glow` |
| Colour fringing, a lens look | `chromaticAberration` |
| A drop or inner shadow | `dropShadow`, `insetShadow` |
| A warm flare over a cut | `LightLeak` or `Starburst` |

`npx kinetograph effects` lists every effect. See [Effects](/docs/making-videos/effects/), [Color correction](/docs/making-videos/color-correction/) and [Light leaks](/docs/making-videos/light-leaks/).

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Grade the photo warmer with a little more contrast, then add grain at a low strength. Keep the headline text clean: grade only the photo layer. Show frame 0 before and after.
```

An effect works on the whole layer it sits on, so put the grade on the picture and the text over it stays clean. A glow or shadow reaches past the layer, so leave a margin. To grade a background and leave a person alone, supply the person as a cut-out with alpha and ask for two layers. Pick one rough look, such as halftone or grain, and say why; several at once read as noise.

## Your own shader

A `shader()` effect runs WGSL on its layer, and a `<canvas>` with a WebGL context runs GLSL. A shader written for a browser usually runs as it is. See [Shaders](/docs/making-videos/shaders/).

## Looks from plugins

Four plugins add looks you can ask for by name. Say which plugin, so the agent reads its `SKILL.md`.

- **[VJ](/docs/plugins/vj/):** screen and sensor looks such as `crt`, `vhs` and `thermal`, and boxes and links drawn on whatever moves.
- **[Paper](/docs/plugins/paper/):** turns a picture with a transparent background into a paper cut-out, and unfolds it from a crumpled packet.
- **[Hexed](/docs/plugins/hexed/):** rolls a grade, glitch, grain, shake or print look from a seed. The same number gives the same look.
- **[Kinetic typography](/docs/plugins/kinetic-typography/):** text that moves with the voice. See [Text, captions and code](/docs/tips/text-captions-code/).

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Use the paper plugin. Show public/images/me.png, which has a transparent background, as a CutOut with a torn edge on kraft stock, and have it unfold with FoldOut at 0:01. Show a strip.
```

## Supplied art

Kinetograph draws; it doesn't generate pictures. Put your artwork in `public/images/` and name the files, then ask the agent to animate it: a slow push-in, parallax between cut-outs, a card flip. Use PNG with alpha for cut-outs, and SVG for shapes and icons.

## 3D

Ask for what you want to see. Kinetograph builds it with its own 3D layer and has no Three.js behind it.

- **A flat card turning:** "Flip the card over with a 3D turn and a camera." Good for a card, a phone or a poster.
- **A real scene:** a `Scene3D` with a camera, lights, shapes, glTF and GLB models and 3D text.

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Add a 960 by 540 3D scene on a dark floor: public/models/phone.glb turning 360 degrees over 6 seconds, one key light from the upper left that casts a shadow, a slow camera push-in. Run doctor and tell me if the model uses anything the engine can't read.
```

Say where the camera starts and ends, how many lights and where, and the model's path and turn. A Three.js project needs to be rebuilt as a `Scene3D`: use `/kinetograph-migrate`. See [Transforms](/docs/making-videos/transforms/#3d-scenes) and [What works](/docs/compat/#3d).
