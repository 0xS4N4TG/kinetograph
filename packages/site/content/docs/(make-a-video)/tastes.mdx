title: Tastes
description: A taste is a reusable style for a reel. See the ones that ship, use one, or make your own from a video.
---

A taste is a whole style: the palette, fonts and textures, how text enters, how the camera moves, how shots cut, how pictures are treated, and the sound. It is written down as `TASTE.md` (for people and agents) and often `taste.json` (the numbers), so every reel in it looks like part of one series.

## The tastes that ship

| Taste | Look | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| [Archive](/tastes/archive/) | Period photographs drift in layers under film flicker and dust, with typed dates and captions | History, biography |
| [Collage](/tastes/collage/) | Paper cut-outs and torn labels over an old newspaper page, in stop motion | History hooks, documentary openers |
| [Explainer](/tastes/explainer/) | Cut-out objects on textured paper under big type, one colour for the key word, quick wipes | Business lessons, one-idea brand reels |
| [Papercut](/tastes/papercut/) | A toy-like paper diorama of cut-paper characters and props, with the camera circling the set | Tech and AI explainers |
| [Print](/tastes/print/) | Flat inks print in turn over paper, with a dot screen that jumps and slight misregistration | Art, design, culture |
| [Vox style](/tastes/vox-style/) | Tinted, halftoned photos on grid paper, caps that fill yellow, numbers that roll like an odometer | History and science explainers |

`pale-whip.ts` in [kinetograph-ui](/docs/kinetograph-ui/) is a seventh: flat app screens on pale grey with a heavy whip blur. It is not installable with `add`. Each taste has a demo reel in its folder.

## Use one

Install it into your project, then point the agent at it:

```sh
npx kinetograph add archive
```

The taste lands in `src/tastes/archive/`, with its `TASTE.md`, components and media.

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Make a 20 second vertical video about the first photographs in the taste at src/tastes/archive/. Read TASTE.md first. The words are "Hold still".
```

## Make your own

Give the [taste skill](/docs/agents/taste-clone/) a reference video and ask for its taste. It keeps the style and leaves out the content, and writes `TASTE.md` and `taste.json`. A taste written after a match that passed its checks is measured; one written from watching alone is a guess. See [Recreating something you saw](/docs/tips/matching/).

To share one, see [Contributing to the registry](/docs/contributing/registry/).
