---
title: Taste skill
description: The kinetograph-taste-clone skill matches a reference reel shot for shot, or lifts its style into a taste.
---

`kinetograph-taste-clone` gives an agent a reel or reference video and a way to build from it without guessing. It reads the video frame by frame, writes down measurements, builds from those numbers, and checks each shot against the reference side by side before it shows you anything.

## Install

It comes from [kinetograph-ui](/docs/kinetograph-ui/) (MIT), not from the Kinetograph repo:

```sh
npx skills add 0xS4N4TG/kinetograph-ui
```

It needs `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe` and `python3`, plus `yt-dlp` to fetch a link. It works with Kinetograph, Remotion or any frame-based renderer.

## What to ask for

| You want | Mode |
|---|---|
| A video with your words and objects, and the reference's timing, camera, layout, type and transitions | Match (the default) |
| The reference's style, to reuse in new videos | Taste |
| Both | Match, then Taste |
| A component like a chart, UI demo or audio visual you saw | Component |
| A drawn look: hand lettering, scribbles, cut paper | Trace |

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-taste-clone Here is a reel: <url>. Match it with our words and logo, then save its taste as src/tastes/ledger/.
```

When it is unclear, say Match first.

## What it does

1. **Fetch and read.** It downloads the video (Pinterest, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube or any `yt-dlp` link) into a git-ignored folder, then reads every frame and finds the cuts and where each move starts and settles.
2. **Write a spec.** `SPEC.md` has a number for every timing, position, colour, shadow and blur. Anything it only estimated is marked and measured before the build relies on it.
3. **Choose assets.** Each object is picked for its slot and cut out cleanly, from public-domain or CC0 sources, and credited in `CREDITS.md`.
4. **Build from the spec.** One shot at a time, with the spec's keyframes and no engine default standing in for a measured look.
5. **Check every shot.** Timing within a frame, then layout, colour, type, shadows, edges, glow, blur, transitions and assets, each shown as reference, result and difference at full size. You see the video only when the checks pass, plus a side-by-side video and a list of what differs on purpose.
6. **Write the taste** (Taste mode): `TASTE.md` and `taste.json`, with frame numbers turned into durations and ratios and this reel's words and objects left out.

The checks grow as new misses are found, so the skill's own folder is the list to read.

In Trace mode the skill vectorises the frames into a private draft that is only used for checking. It is never shipped or committed. A piece you ship draws your own content in that hand. Trace also needs `potrace` and PIL.

## Good to know

- The reference's own text, logos and people are always swapped for yours.
- "Flat" or "not accurate" tells it nothing. Name a frame and a number: "the shadow under the card at 0:03 is too hard".
- Downloaded media is never committed.
