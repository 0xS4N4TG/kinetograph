Kinetograph turns an idea into a finished video. You ask your coding agent for the video you want, and Kinetograph draws it and saves an MP4.

<Video name="what-is-kinetograph" label="A montage of short videos made with Kinetograph" caption="Short videos made with Kinetograph: a launch film, a paper-cut reel, a type spot and a 3D scene." />

## What can it make?

Title cards, moving text, captions, charts, photo slideshows, paper-cut looks and glitch effects. It also makes 3D scenes and shader effects. [See examples](/docs/examples/).

## Three things to know

**An agent can build it.** You describe the video. Your agent writes it, looks at the frames, fixes what is off and saves the MP4. You do not need to write code on this path.

**You can edit it.** The video is a folder of plain files. Studio opens it with a live preview, a timeline and a chat with your agent.

**It comes out the same every time.** A frame depends only on its number, so rendering twice gives the same video.

## Good to know

- You need Node.js 22 or newer.
- Nothing in drawing needs a browser.
- Already have a Remotion or HyperFrames project? It renders as it is. See [Bring a project](/docs/making/bring-a-project/).
