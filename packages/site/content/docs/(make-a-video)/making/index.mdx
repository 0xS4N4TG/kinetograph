Pick what you want to make. Each guide shows a finished video and the prompts that made it.

**Want a ready-made start?** The [library](/docs/library/use-an-item/) has whole videos and single pieces, such as a chart. You add one and change it.

**Not sure which?** Describe the whole video in your own words. Your agent works out the kind from what you ask for.
