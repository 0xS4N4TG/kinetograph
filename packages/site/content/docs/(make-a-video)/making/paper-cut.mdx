Ask your agent to use Paper. You get keyword match cuts, photo cut-outs and hand-drawn lines on torn paper.

## Make it

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Use the paper plugin to make a 6 second paper-cut video. Cut out the photo in public/images/me.png (its background is already removed) on kraft paper with torn edges, and unfold it from a crumpled packet. Then draw a wobbly hand-drawn line round it. Look at the frames, then save it to out/paper.mp4.
```

## Ask for one of three things

- **Keyword match cuts:** a highlighted word holds still while the page behind it changes.
- **Cut-outs:** a photo with its background already removed, which can unfold from a crumpled packet.
- **Hand-drawn lines:** a marker, pencil or brush line round a moving subject.

Paper has five stocks (white, newsprint, kraft, binder and cardboard) and four edge styles (torn, cut, wavy and sticker).

## Prompts that work

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Use the paper plugin for a match cut on the word "Super Bowl": 10 cuts a second for 3 seconds, on newsprint, with the word highlighted yellow. Use my screenshots in public/images/shots.
```

### Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **The stock and the edge** | "Kraft with torn edges" picks the look. |
| **The keyword, for a match cut** | "Super Bowl" is marked on every page. |
| **Your own pictures** | Name the files in `public/images/`. A cut-out needs a picture whose background is already removed. |
| **How fast the cuts go** | "10 cuts a second". |

### Variants

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A line round a moving subject">
    Ask for a marker, pencil, crayon, scribble or brush line, drawn round a subject that moves, on torn paper. [Scrapline cut-outs](/catalog/scrapline/) is a template that does this.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Paper sounds">
    Ask for a paper flick on every cut, a crinkle as the packet opens and a soft landing for each cut-out. Paper makes these sounds in code, with no files.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Common mistakes

**Asking for "paper" and nothing else.** The look has five stocks and four edges.
- **Avoid:** "make it look like paper"
- **Instead:** "kraft paper, torn edges"

## Review the result

- The paper stock and edges are the ones you asked for.
- Every cut-out shows its whole subject, with no hard fringe.
- The keyword is readable on every page.
