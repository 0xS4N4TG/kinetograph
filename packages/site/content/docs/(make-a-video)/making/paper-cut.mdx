## Make it

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Use the paper plugin to make a 6 second paper-cut video. Cut out the photo in public/images/me.png (its background is already removed) on kraft paper with torn edges, and unfold it from a crumpled packet. Then draw a wobbly hand-drawn line round it. Look at the frames, then save it to out/paper.mp4.
```

## Looks

- **Five paper stocks:** white, newsprint, kraft, binder and cardboard.
- **Four edge styles:** torn, cut, wavy and sticker.
- **Keyword match cuts:** a highlighted word holds still while the page behind it changes.
- **Cut-outs:** a photo with its background already removed, which can unfold from a crumpled packet.
- **Hand-drawn lines:** a marker, pencil or brush line round a moving subject.

## Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **The stock and the edge** | "Kraft with torn edges" picks the look. |
| **The keyword, for a match cut** | "Super Bowl" is marked on every page. |
| **Your own pictures** | Name the files in `public/images/`. A cut-out needs a picture whose background is already removed. |
| **How fast the cuts go** | "10 cuts a second". |

## Common mistakes

**Asking for "paper" and nothing else.** The look has five stocks and four edges.
- **Avoid:** "make it look like paper"
- **Instead:** "kraft paper, torn edges"
