Bring a voice file, a track and a subtitle file. Tell your agent where each one is. You get a video with sound and words on screen.

<Video name="voice-music-captions" label="A clip with a voiceover, music under it and captions that light up word by word" caption="A clip with voice, music and captions that light up as each word is said." />

## Make it

Put the voice file in `public/audio/` and the track beside it. Put the subtitle file in `public/captions/`. Then paste:

```text title="Suggested prompt"
The narration is public/audio/vo.wav and its subtitles are public/captions/vo.srt. Start the narration at 1 second. Put public/audio/bed.mp3 under the whole video at 30% volume, lowered to 12% while the narration plays. Show 3 to 5 words at a time in the lower third, white on a 60% black bar, and light the word being said in yellow. Look at the frames, then save it to out/video.mp4.
```

## Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **What each file is and what it says** | Your agent can place a reveal on a word: "the third card appears on 'Search', at 0:05.4". |
| **How loud the music sits** | "30% under the voice" keeps the voice clear. |
| **The beat times, for a track with a beat** | Cuts can land on each hit. |
| **How many words show at a time** | Captions stay readable. |
| **One caption style** | Keep it the same for the whole video. |

## Sound effects

Tie each effect to a moment, such as "a click when each card lands". Ask for the three or four that matter. Use your own files. Or ask for a click, a whoosh or a hit made in code.

## Check the mix

Say what you hear: "the music drowns the voice at 0:12". Then ask your agent to run `audio analyze`. It reports loudness, peaks, clipped samples and silences.

## Common mistakes

**Expecting speech or timings to be made for you.** Kinetograph does not turn text into speech or speech into words. Bring a voice file and a subtitle file, or word timings from your speech-to-text tool.
- **Avoid:** "add a voiceover that reads my script"
- **Instead:** "the narration is public/audio/vo.wav"

**Mixing caption styles.**
- **Avoid:** "a different caption look for each line"
- **Instead:** "one look for the whole video"

For captions that move with the voice, ask for Kinetic typography. It has fifteen named looks.
