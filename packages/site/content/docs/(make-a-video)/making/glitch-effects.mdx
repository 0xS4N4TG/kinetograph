Ask your agent for a smeared cut, a random look or a tape look. You get a clip that glitches as you described. It renders the same every time.

## Make it

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Cut from public/video/clouds.mp4 into public/video/pig.mp4 with a datamosh: for the first 45 frames of the pig clip, let its motion drag the pixels of the clouds shot. Then play the pig clip as it is. Look at the frames, then save it to out/glitch.mp4.
```

A datamosh is what you see when a video loses its keyframe. The old picture stays on screen and the next shot's movement pushes it around.

## Ask for one of three things

- **Datamosh.** The next shot's motion drags the last picture of the old one. See [Datamosh](/docs/making-videos/datamosh/).
- **Random look.** Hexed picks a look from a number. It fills eleven slots, such as crop, shake, grade and grunge. The same number gives the same look. See [Hexed](/docs/plugins/hexed/).
- **Tape and screen looks.** VJ adds a CRT, a VHS tape, one-bit dither or a thermal palette. See [VJ](/docs/plugins/vj/).

## Prompts that work

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Use the hexed plugin on public/video/clip.mp4 with seed 42. Keep the sepia grade, turn the mirror off, and apply it at 70% strength. Save a draft to out/look.mp4.
```

### Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Which two clips** | The smear needs the clip it leaves and the clip it enters. |
| **How many frames** | "45 frames" sets how long the smear lasts. |
| **A number for a rolled look** | "Seed 42" brings the same look back. |
| **Keep one slot, drop another** | "Keep the sepia grade, no mirror". |

### Variants

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A tape look">
    Ask for the VJ plugin's VHS look: line wobble, smeared colour, a head-switch tear, snow and dropouts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A CRT screen">
    Ask for curved glass, scanlines, static and dark corners, and for the picture to fold to a dot at the end.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Common mistakes

**Calling it "glitchy".** Name the effect.
- **Avoid:** "make the cut glitchy"
- **Instead:** "a datamosh of 45 frames into the pig clip"

**Re-rolling a look you liked.**
- **Avoid:** "try another look"
- **Instead:** "keep seed 42 and change only the grade"

<Note>
  A datamosh needs clips saved as H.264 or MPEG-4. Any other clip plays without the effect, and a warning says so. A smear lasts 600 frames at most.
</Note>

## Review the result

- The smear starts and ends on the frames you named.
- The old picture is the one you expected.
- The look is the same on a second render.
