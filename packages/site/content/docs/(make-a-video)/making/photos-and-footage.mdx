Put your photos and clips in a folder. Tell your agent how long each stays and how it moves. You get a slideshow or a short cut.

## Make it

Copy your files into `public/images/` for photos and `public/video/` for clips. Then paste:

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Make a 15 second video from the photos in public/images. Show each for 3 seconds with a slow push-in, and dissolve between them. End on public/video/clip.mp4, from 2 seconds to 5. Put the title "Summer" over the first photo. Look at the frames, then save it to out/summer.mp4.
```

Your agent lays out the photos, trims the clip and checks the frames. Then it saves the MP4.

## How a good slideshow is built

- **One idea per picture.** Each photo gets the same time on screen, unless you say otherwise.
- **One move.** A slow push-in, a pan or a hold. Mixing moves makes it busy.
- **A clear start and end.** A title over the first photo, and a clip or a closing card to finish.

<Warning>
  A render reads files on your computer only. A remote web address is an error, so download each file into the project first.
</Warning>

## Prompts that work

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Make a 20 second slideshow of the photos in public/images in the order of their file names. Show each for 4 seconds. Pan left to right on the wide ones and push in on the others. Dissolve between them. Put "Summer 2026" at the bottom left of the last one.
```

### Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Where the files are** | Name the folder or each file. |
| **How long each stays** | "3 seconds each" sets the length of the video. |
| **How it moves** | "Slow push-in", "pan left", "hold still". |
| **How to fit the frame** | "Fill the frame, cropping the edges" or "show the whole picture". |
| **Which part of a clip** | "From 2 seconds to 5" trims it. A clip can also be sped up, looped or muted. |

### Common mistakes

**Using a web address.** A render reads files on your computer only.
- **Avoid:** "use the photo at https://example.com/a.jpg"
- **Instead:** "download it into public/images first, then use it"

**Leaving the order to chance.**
- **Avoid:** "use all my photos"
- **Instead:** "show them in the order of their file names"

## Present it

Save the MP4 with `npx kinetograph render src/index.tsx`. Add `--draft` first for a half-size look at the timing.
