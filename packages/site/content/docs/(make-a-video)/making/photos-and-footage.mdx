Put your photos and clips in a folder and tell your agent how long each one stays and how it moves. You get a slideshow or a short cut.

<Video name="photos-and-footage" label="Four photos in turn, each with a slow push-in, then a clip" caption="A slideshow with a slow push-in on each photo, ending on a clip." silent />

## Make it

Copy your files into `public/images/` for photos and `public/video/` for clips. Then paste:

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Make a 15 second video from the photos in public/images. Show each for 3 seconds with a slow push-in, and dissolve between them. End on public/video/clip.mp4, from 2 seconds to 5. Put the title "Summer" over the first photo. Look at the frames, then save it to out/summer.mp4.
```

Your agent lays out the photos, trims the clip, checks the frames and saves the MP4.

## Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Where the files are** | Name the folder or each file. |
| **How long each stays** | "3 seconds each" sets the length of the video. |
| **How it moves** | "Slow push-in", "pan left", "hold still". |
| **How to fit the frame** | "Fill the frame, cropping the edges" or "show the whole picture". |
| **Which part of a clip** | "From 2 seconds to 5" trims it. A clip can also be sped up, looped or muted. |

## Common mistakes

**Using a web address.** A render reads files on your computer only. A remote URL is an error.
- **Avoid:** `use the photo at https://example.com/a.jpg`
- **Instead:** `download it into public/images first, then use it`

**Leaving the order to chance.**
- **Avoid:** `use all my photos`
- **Instead:** `show them in the order of their file names`
