title: Make a title card or moving text
description: Get a title card, a headline or words that move on screen. Say the words, the look and when they arrive.
---

<Video name="title-cards-and-text" label="A headline writes on, then three words slide up one after another" caption="A headline that writes on, then words that slide up." />

Tell your agent the exact words, the font and when each line arrives. You get a title card or a headline that moves the way you described.

## Make it

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Make a 5 second title card with the words "Ship it on Friday" in Inter 700, white on a dark blue background. Fit it on two lines inside 900 px, 120 px from the top. It arrives at 1 second and leaves at 4. Look at the frames, run lint, then save it to out/title.mp4.
```

Your agent writes the card, checks that the text fits and saves the MP4. Change it by asking, for example, "make the headline bigger".

## Pick the kind of motion

- **Write on**, one letter at a time over 20 frames.
- **Slide up**, each word 3 frames after the last.
- **Type** with a caret, the way code appears.
- **Hold and fade** for a calm title.

Over busy footage, ask for a dark bar or a shadow behind the text.

## Prompts that work

When you want to set the look yourself:

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Show the headline "Ship it on Friday" in Inter 700, white. Each word slides up from a 20 px offset, 3 frames apart. Put a 60% black bar behind it. Hold the finished headline for 2 seconds.
```

### Say these when they matter

| Say | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **The words in quotes** | Unquoted copy gets reworded. |
| **A font you can load** | Use a Google Fonts name or a file in `public/fonts/`. With none, text falls back to the bundled DM Sans. |
| **Size and place** | "120 px, centred, 80 px from the top" beats "big". |
| **When it arrives and leaves** | "In at 0:01, out at 0:04". |
| **A number for contrast** | "White on a 60% black bar" beats "make it readable". |

<Tip>
  The words, the font and the timing are the three cheapest things to state. Leave the rest to your agent.
</Tip>

### Variants

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A lower third">
    Say what it says, when it enters and leaves, and where: "a lower third 'Mara Okafor, Founder' at the bottom left, in at 0:03, out at 0:08". To lay it over other footage, ask for it as its own video with a transparent background.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Code on screen">
    Paste the code and say it must be shown exactly. Choose the motion: typing with a caret, a highlight on lines 4 to 6, a diff in red and green, or a scroll. Name a monospace font and colours for keywords, strings and comments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Words cut to a beat">
    Give the beat times in seconds and ask for one word per hit. [Words cut to the beat](/templates/word-flash/) is a template that does this.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Common mistakes

**Leaving the copy unquoted.** Your agent may reword it.
- **Avoid:** "a title about shipping on Friday"
- **Instead:** "the title 'Ship it on Friday'"

**Skipping the check.** Text can run past its box or the edge of the picture.
- **Avoid:** "render it"
- **Instead:** "run lint, fix what it reports, then render"

## Review the result

- Every word is the one you quoted.
- The text is readable on its background for the whole time it is on screen.
- Nothing runs past the edge of the picture.
- Each line arrives and leaves when you said.
