[Studio](/docs/terminology/studio/) is a local editor. Open a project in it, look at the preview, change props, pick a layer or a span of time and ask your coding agent to change it. The engine draws the preview frames, so what you see is what a render makes.

Studio opens a browser at `http://127.0.0.1:3000`, rebuilds the project when a file changes, and runs until you press Ctrl-C. The [CLI reference](/docs/reference/cli/#studio) lists the options.

## Where to start

| You want to change | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Something you can see in this frame | Click it in the preview, then the Props tab |
| When something appears or how long it stays | The timeline |
| A setting, such as a colour or a word | The Props tab, then save a preset |
| The story, or several scenes | The chat. Mark up the preview and send your notes |
| The final file | Export |

## The window

- **Agent** on the left: the chat with your coding agent.
- **Preview** in the middle, with the timeline under it.
- **Inspector** on the right, with the Props, Scene and Renders tabs.
- **Top bar:** the composition switcher, the command menu (Cmd+K, or Ctrl+K elsewhere, lists every action and shortcut) and the Export button.

<Media src="studio/command-menu-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The command menu open over the window, listing the actions of Studio."} />

Space plays and pauses, and the arrow keys step a frame. Click a layer to select it, or drag a box around several.

## The preview

Type a time into the timecode box (`2.4`, `mm:ss` or `mm:ss.ff`) to jump there. If the composition has audio, the preview plays it.

### Mark up the preview

To show the agent what to change, press `M`. Then drop a **pin**, drag a **box**, draw with the **pen**, or drag a **range** on the timeline ruler. Type a comment, press Enter to add the note, and send the notes when you are done. The agent sees each note with its frame and the layers under your mark, and a picture of the frame with your marks drawn on it.

## The timeline

The arrow at the left of the timeline opens one row per layer. Click the ruler to move the playhead, or drag across it to pick a span of time. Click a clip to pick its span. The span goes to the agent with your next message, with a contact sheet of the frames in it.

<Media src="studio/timeline-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The timeline expanded, with a film strip of frames, one row for each Sequence and the narration clips under it, and the playhead on the print shot."} />

## Props and presets

The Props tab draws a form from the composition's zod `schema`: a slider for a number, a switch for a boolean, a colour input for `zColor()`. With no schema, each field is inferred from its default. **Reset** returns every prop to the defaults, and your edits are kept in the browser.

<Media src="studio/props-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The Props tab with a form drawn from the composition schema: text fields, a colour input, a slider, a switch and a select."} />

**Save preset** in the Presets menu saves the props you see as `presets/<composition id>/<name>.json`. You can commit it, and `--props` renders it from the command line. See [Plugins](/docs/plugins/#presets).

A field that a [plugin](/docs/plugins/) draws its own [panel](/docs/studio/panels/#plugin-panels) for shows as a card in place of the form. The **Plugins** list at the bottom shows what the project uses and what else you can add.

<Media src="studio/plugins-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The Plugins list at the bottom of the Props tab, with the plugins the site offers and their status."} />

## The Scene and Renders tabs

**Scene** is the tree of layers at the playhead. **Renders** is the queue of exports, with progress and the output file.

<Media src="studio/renders-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The Renders tab: the queue of exports with progress and the output file."} />

## Export

**Export** opens a dialog for the composition. Pick the whole video or a range, an output path (`.mp4` or `.mov`), and **Draft** for a half-size render that checks timing. The export uses the props you edited.

<Media src="studio/export-dark.webp" width={1600} height={1160} alt={"The Export dialog: whole video or a custom range, the output path and the draft switch."} />

## The agent

The chat on the left talks to your coding agent. Each message carries the selection: the composition, and the frame or span. See [Studio's agent chat](/docs/agents/studio-agent/). When the agent needs a decision from you, it opens a box in the window: see [Panels](/docs/studio/panels/).
