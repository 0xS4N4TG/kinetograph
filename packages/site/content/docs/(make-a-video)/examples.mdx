## Start from a template

Each template is a whole video you can add to your project and change. Each prompt adds one and swaps in your own words. Replace the words in quotes.

### A type spot cut to the beat

[Words cut to the beat](/catalog/word-flash/) is a black-and-white spot where every hit of the track shows one word.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the word-flash template to my project. Use my own track and change the words to "make", "it", "move". Save a draft to out/spot.mp4.
```

### A launch film

[Launch film](/catalog/launch-film/) runs twenty seconds, from a typed headline to the final mark.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the launch-film template. Change the headline to "Ship it Friday" and the final mark to my logo in public/images/logo.png. Look at the frames, then save it.
```

### A paper cut-out

[Scrapline cut-outs](/catalog/scrapline/) draws a hand-drawn border round a cut-out subject on torn paper.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the scrapline template and put my photo in public/images/me.png in the cut-out. Keep the wobbly line.
```

### A grid opener

[Grid opener](/catalog/grid-reel/) lays a 3 by 3 grid over every shot while words land.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the grid-reel template. Use my three clips in public/video and change the words to "new", "season", "out now".
```

### A product on a laptop

[Laptop opening](/catalog/laptop-open/) opens a 3D laptop lid, wakes the screen and moves the camera into the app.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the laptop-open template and show my app screenshot in public/images/app.png on the screen.
```

### A year in review

[Year recap dial](/catalog/recap-dial/) snaps a dial month by month, with a sticker for each.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the recap-dial template and fill each month with one line about my year. Use the 12 lines I paste next.
```

### A history explainer

[History explainer](/catalog/dostoevsky/) mixes slow moves over old maps and taped photos on kraft paper.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the dostoevsky template and replace the script with a 40 second history of the printing press. Use public-domain images only.
```

### A chat prompt and its reply

[Chat prompt to reply](/catalog/prompt-reply/) types a prompt into a box, sends it and cuts to the reply.

```text title="Suggested prompt"
Add the prompt-reply template. The prompt is "What is a kinetograph?" and the reply is two short lines I will paste next.
```

## Single pieces

Components are single pieces you drop into a video. Add one by name, for example a chart, a background or a grid:

```sh
npx kinetograph add area-chart
npx kinetograph add aurora
npx kinetograph add grid-rect
```

The [library](/catalog/?c=plugins) shows every template and component with a clip. [Use a library item](/docs/library/use-an-item/) covers how to add one.
