---
title: Studio
description: The local editor that previews a project, with a timeline, a props form and a chat with your coding agent.
---

Studio is a local editor in your browser. The engine draws its preview frames, so what you see is what a render makes.

```sh
npx kinetograph studio src/index.tsx
```

It has a preview that plays sound, an Export button, and an inspector with the Props, Scene and Renders tabs. Cmd+K opens a menu of every action.

The timeline sits under the preview. It shows one row per layer, with `Sequence` clips coloured by kind. Drag across it to pick a span of time to ask your agent about.

**Coming from Remotion:** `remotion studio` becomes `kinetograph studio <entry>`.
**Coming from HyperFrames:** `hyperframes preview` becomes `kinetograph studio my-video`.
