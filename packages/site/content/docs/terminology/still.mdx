---
title: Still
description: A single frame saved as a PNG, or a composition that is only one frame long.
---

Still has two meanings, and both are about one frame.

The `still` command renders one frame to a PNG. It is the fastest way to look at your work.

```sh
npx kinetograph still src/index.tsx 15 -o out/card-15.png
```

The `<Still>` component declares a composition that has exactly one frame. It takes the same props as `<Composition>`, without `durationInFrames`. Its `fps` defaults to 30.

Next to `still`, `strip` makes a sheet of evenly spaced frames and `onion` blends a range of frames to show a motion path.

**Coming from Remotion:** the same `<Still>` and `remotion still`.
**Coming from HyperFrames:** no equivalent. Use `kinetograph still index.html 15 -o frame.png`.
