## How it was measured

One Apple M4 (10 cores), macOS 26.5.2, Node v26.7.0, on 2026-11-09. Kinetograph at commit `3eb93eaa`, HyperFrames 0.9.26 and Remotion 4.0.542. Each cell is one warm-up render and then 3 timed renders; the tables show the median and the min–max of the 3. Before every run the 1-minute load average was under 3.0 and the machine was otherwise idle. The time is the whole `render` command, start to finished H.264 file. Kinetograph uses its default 4 workers, Remotion `--concurrency 5` with its headless shell, and HyperFrames the defaults of `hyperframes render` with its headless Chrome shell. fps is frames per second of wall time.

A ratio is the other engine's median divided by Kinetograph's. The range in brackets is best against worst and worst against best of the 3 runs each. Ratios are per scene; there is no single number for the engine.

## HyperFrames pages with a Remotion port

Each scene is a HyperFrames page. Kinetograph runs that same page through its HyperFrames runtime. The Remotion version is a port.

### film

The Remotion port rewrites the GSAP tweens as frame maths and bakes the geometry; the cosmic orb is the same WebGL shader. No version has audio.

| engine | wall s (median) | min–max | fps | peak RSS MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetograph | 10.26 | 10.17–10.28 | 175.4 | 2708 |
| HyperFrames 0.9.26 | 23.25 | 23.08–23.70 | 77.4 | 3810 |
| Remotion 4.0.542 | 35.94 | 35.89–36.24 | 50.1 | 3946 |

Kinetograph: 2.3× (2.2–2.3×) vs HyperFrames 0.9.26; 3.5× (3.5–3.6×) vs Remotion 4.0.542.

## Remotion projects

Both engines run the same Remotion project, unchanged.

### compat

The `examples/compat` project in this repo.

| engine | wall s (median) | min–max | fps | peak RSS MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetograph | 1.09 | 1.08–1.09 | 138.1 | 1227 |
| Remotion 4.0.542 | 4.26 | 4.24–4.28 | 35.2 | 2470 |

Kinetograph: 3.9× (3.9–4.0×) vs Remotion 4.0.542.

### papercut

A Remotion project from a private corpus (988 frames, 1080x1920).

| engine | wall s (median) | min–max | fps | peak RSS MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetograph | 20.46 | 20.45–20.48 | 48.3 | 3240 |
| Remotion 4.0.542 | 646.13 | 644.35–654.40 | 1.5 | 10599 |

Kinetograph: 31.6× (31.5–32.0×) vs Remotion 4.0.542.

Read this ratio with its cause. Remotion held a steady 1.5 fps from the first frame, and swap stayed flat, so the time is Chrome's own. The project layers full-frame SVG paper-grain filters (`feTurbulence`, `feDisplacementMap`) and drop-shadows, which Chrome works out on the CPU. The gap was not profiled further than that.

## Not measured

- Remotion versions of dm1, dm2, dm5, mg4, mg5 and pr1: there is no port.
- HyperFrames on compat and papercut: they are Remotion projects.
- Steady-state fps of HyperFrames and Remotion. These tables give wall time only, which is what you wait for.
- Any machine other than this M4.
