## Worker scaling

A render splits the video between workers, and they draw different parts at the same time. On this machine, 4 workers are <Fact k="gain" /> faster than 1 worker, not 4× faster.

<WorkerScaling />

Two things hold it back today:

- **Replay.** A worker has to play the animation through the frames other workers drew before it can draw its own. That is <Fact k="replay" /> of a worker's time.
- **Slow cores.** One worker already keeps most of the four fast cores busy. Extra workers land on slow cores, and each frame takes <Fact k="slow" /> longer there.

<WorkerLanes />

Machines with more equal cores have more room to scale. This is being worked on.
