Still has two meanings, and both are about one frame.

The `still` command renders one frame to a PNG. It is the fastest way to look at your work. Without `-o` the file is `out/stills/<comp>-f<frame>.png`.

The `<Still>` component declares a composition that has exactly one frame. It takes the same props as `<Composition>`, without `durationInFrames`. Its `fps` defaults to 30. A HyperFrames page has no equivalent.

Next to `still`, `strip` makes a sheet of evenly spaced frames and `onion` blends a range of frames to show a motion path.
