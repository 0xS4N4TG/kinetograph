---
title: Component
description: A ready-made piece listed on the Kinetograph site, which you add to your own project.
---

The site lists components: ready-made pieces such as grids, carousels, charts, backgrounds and effects. Each has its own page under `/components/<id>/`, with its props read from its zod schema.

A component's page lists the props you can change and the [plugins](/docs/terminology/plugin/) it uses.

The earliest ones were called templates.

Do not confuse this with a React component, which is any function that returns elements. The ones on the site are the ready-made ones.
