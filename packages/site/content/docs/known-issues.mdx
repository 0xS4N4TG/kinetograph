Gaps and differences you may hit when a project renders, with what happens in each case. For what is supported, see [Remotion compatibility](/docs/compat/).

## Remotion APIs

- **Effects on some elements.** `createEffect` on `Solid`, `CanvasImage`, `AnimatedImage` and `HtmlInCanvas` is exported, but the effect is left out, and a warning is printed once. The `onPaint` and `onInit` props of `HtmlInCanvas` are not supported. Both need an element drawn into a canvas, and a GPU effect pass, which are not built yet.
- **IFrame.** `IFrame` is not drawn. An iframe is reported as unsupported content.
- **Not exported.** `Internals`, `FolderContext`, `PathInternals` and `AudioForPreview` are not exported. They are internal to Remotion.
- **Partly supported.** `webcodecs` video and audio encode and decode, and the box tree and sample callbacks of `media-parser`, throw `UnsupportedApiError`. They need a video decoder and encoder in the addon, which is not built.
- **Not planned.** `@remotion/three` and `@remotion/player` are replaced by the engine's own 3D layer and studio. `renderer`, `bundler`, `cli` and `lambda` are replaced by the Kinetograph CLI. `tailwind` and `tailwind-v4` are webpack configuration only. Tailwind itself works.

## Engine features not finished

- **Canvas 2D.** A pattern of a canvas, used for a stroke or for text, is drawn transparent. Fills work, up to 10,000 tiles. `isPointInStroke` ignores caps, joins and dashes. `url()` filters are reported and not drawn. Shadow and filter images are capped at 50 million pixels per canvas layer per frame, and a frame past that is an error.
- **WebGL.** Not supported: renderbuffers; depth and stencil attachments; `COLOR_ATTACHMENT1` and up; a texture level above 0 as a target; targets that are not RGBA or that hold uploaded pixels; `generateMipmap` and `texSubImage2D` on a texture drawn into; `readPixels`, which is refused by design; `blitFramebuffer`, which is reported as unsupported; points and lines; instancing; cube, 3D and float textures; and multisampled edges. Each needs new GPU work. A framebuffer texture starts every frame blank. A frame that only draws into framebuffers still clears the canvas, where a browser would keep the last picture.
- **3D (`scene3d`).** Occlusion and emissive texture maps, bevels and GPU skinning are not supported. The CPU deforms morphs and skins once per pose. `doctor` does not report `three` imports yet. At most 4 lights cast shadows and 8 shadow maps are drawn in all, and a point light takes 6 of them.
- **CSS.** `background-repeat: space` with thousands of gradient tiles still errors. The other repeats are drawn as one tile repeated by `feTile`. Backdrop blur and glow are capped at 128 px, and the `blur` effect takes up to 512.
- **WGSL `shader()`.** Sees only its own layer, runs one pass, has no other texture, and takes `f32` uniforms only.
- **`@remotion/skia` and `@shopify/react-native-skia`.** `Atlas`, `Patch`, `Glyphs`, `TextBlob`, `Picture` and `ImageSVG` are drawn; text inside an SVG is not. Not supported: Reanimated values, `FitBox`, `Skia.Surface` and `Skia.Offscreen`, the `Skia.Image`, `Skia.Data` and `Skia.Typeface` factories, `useVideo`, animated images, paragraph measurement, and shaders or filters set on a `Skia.Paint()` object. The raster is CPU only. PNGs of drawn frames are kept under `~/.cache/kinetograph/skia`, the newest 400 to 600 of them. See [AUTHORING.md](https://github.com/0xS4N4TG/kinetograph/blob/main/docs/AUTHORING.md), section 7.7.
- **`@remotion/rive`.** `onLoad`, `assetLoader`, `effects` and the `ref` getters are not supported. A state machine starts with its inputs at their defaults, and there is no way to set them. Only pictures embedded in the file are supported. See [AUTHORING.md](https://github.com/0xS4N4TG/kinetograph/blob/main/docs/AUTHORING.md), section 7.6.

## Rendering differences

- **preserve-3d with intersecting planes.** Groups with `transform-style: preserve-3d` under `perspective` are sorted in painter's order, with no depth buffer. Planes that intersect each other are drawn wrong.
- **Inline-block with a stacking context is painted twice.** A `display: inline-block` element that also has a `transform`, or any other property that makes it a stacking context or positions it, comes out as two identical layers. Its antialiased edges get double alpha, and it costs two draws. To reproduce it: a div with the text "ab", and a `span` with `display: inline-block; transform: scale(0.5)`. The end card of the launch film (frames 1386 to 1520) has this case.
- **`@paper-design/shaders` is not verified.** The engine has the tree methods this library needs, but the library itself has not been run through it. Its WebGL2 calls, such as `getShaderPrecisionFormat`, are untested.
- **Rare difference with `--concurrency 2`.** In one render out of several, `--concurrency 2` produced a different video from the same project. Other runs at 2 workers matched, and runs at 1, 3, 4 and 6 workers matched. The cause is not known. It may be a rare race, or a fault in that one run. Renders are meant to be identical at any concurrency, so a difference is a bug.
