---
title: Known issues
description: What doesn't work yet in Kinetograph, and what you see instead.
---

Each item says what doesn't work and what you see instead. For what does work, see [Remotion compatibility](/docs/compat/). Items marked "reported" show up in the warnings and in `doctor`.

## Not planned

- **`IFrame`.** Not drawn, because a real webpage needs a browser. It is reported as unsupported content.
- **Not exported.** `Internals`, `FolderContext`, `PathInternals` and `AudioForPreview` are internal to Remotion.
- **Replaced by Kinetograph's own tools.** `@remotion/three` and `@remotion/player` are replaced by the engine's own 3D layer and studio. `renderer`, `bundler`, `cli` and `lambda` are replaced by the Kinetograph CLI.
- **Tailwind packages.** `tailwind` and `tailwind-v4` are webpack configuration only, so they do nothing here. Tailwind itself works.

## Partly working

### Effects and `HtmlInCanvas`

- `2d` and `webgl2` effects (`createEffect`) run, and so do the `onPaint` and `onInit` props.
- `webgpu` effects, and an `onInit` or `onPaint` that asks for `canvas.getContext('webgpu')`, are drawn without the effect and reported.
- State an effect keeps from `setup` does not carry to the next frame. Each frame runs `setup`, `apply` and `cleanup` again.
- `getImageData` of the source is not supported.
- In an `async` `onPaint` or `onInit`, the part after the first `await` does not run.
- An `<HtmlInCanvas>` inside another is not supported.

### Video and audio encoding and decoding in your own code

`@remotion/webcodecs` is Remotion's wrapper around the browser's WebCodecs API, which encodes and decodes video and audio frames. Its encode and decode functions, and the box tree and sample callbacks of `@remotion/media-parser`, throw `UnsupportedApiError`. They need a video decoder and encoder in the addon, which is not built. Normal `<Video>` and `<Audio>` are not affected.

### HyperFrames projects

- **Three.js.** Not drawn. A page that loads Three.js is reported on every frame.
- **WebGPU.** Shader blocks that use `shaders.iife.js` (`aurora`, `blob`, `ripples`, `swirl` and the like) are drawn natively. See [WebGPU](#webgpu).
- **Import maps and ES modules that import.** Not run. They are reported.
- **Scripts share no top-level `const`.** Each `<script>` runs on its own, so a `const` in one is not visible in the next (a `var` or `window.x` is). Scripts of a mounted composition are wrapped in a function, as in HyperFrames.
- **Selectors that reach into an `<svg>` from outside it.** The engine styles an `<svg>` on its own, so the loader writes the matching rules onto the shapes as inline style once the scripts have run. A class added to a shape later, by a tween or a script, is not restyled. The `font-family` of the page's text is written on the `<svg>` the same way.
- **Timing.** The start of a `<video>` inside a composition is the host's start plus its own; the host's `data-playback-start` and `data-playback-rate` move its timeline, not the media inside. `data-start` references stay inside one composition. `data-hidden`, audio lanes (`data-automation`), `data-color-grading`, audio effects and speed ramps are ignored.
- **CSS.** `html` and `body` rules apply to two wrapper elements. `@media` rules are not applied to selectors that reach into an `<svg>`.
- **Layout read at load.** A page whose length is not declared (`data-duration`) runs its scripts before any layout exists, so a script that measures sees nothing.

### Canvas 2D

- A pattern of a canvas, used for a stroke or for text, is drawn transparent.
- Pattern fills work, up to 10,000 tiles.
- `isPointInStroke` ignores caps, joins and dashes.
- `url()` filters are reported and not drawn.
- Shadow and filter images are capped at 50 million pixels per canvas layer per frame. A frame past that is an error.

### WebGL

A framebuffer texture starts every frame blank. A frame that only draws into framebuffers still clears the canvas, where a browser would keep the last picture. Each item below needs new GPU work.

- Not supported: renderbuffers; depth and stencil attachments; `COLOR_ATTACHMENT1` and up; a texture level above 0 as a target; targets that are not RGBA or that hold uploaded pixels; `generateMipmap` and `texSubImage2D` on a texture drawn into; points and lines; instancing; cube, 3D and float textures; and multisampled edges.
- `readPixels` is refused by design.
- `blitFramebuffer` is reported as unsupported.

### WebGPU

A canvas's `getContext('webgpu')` records, and the render passes it submits are drawn on the engine's own device. Drawn: buffers (uniform, storage, vertex, index), WGSL modules, bind group layouts, render pipelines (blend, topology, culling, vertex buffers, `layout: 'auto'`), bind groups, render passes into the canvas, `draw`, `drawIndexed`, viewport, scissor, `writeBuffer`, `copyBufferToBuffer`.

- Not supported, and reported on every frame (`--strict` fails): textures and samplers (so images and video in a shader), compute pipelines, query sets, render bundles, indirect draws, depth and stencil, multisampling, a render pass that is not into the canvas, and reading a buffer back (`mapAsync`).
- The canvas is drawn as 8-bit `rgba8unorm`; `rgba16float` and a display-p3 `colorSpace` are reported and drawn in sRGB without extended range.
- A WGSL module naga refuses is reported and the canvas draws nothing; one that wgpu refuses later is a warning on stderr and a transparent canvas.
- `shaders.iife.js` effects that read pictures (the ones that take an image or another layer as input) need textures and are reported.

### WGSL `shader()`

It sees only its own layer, runs one pass, has no other texture, and takes `f32` uniforms only.

### 3D (`scene3d`)

- Not supported: occlusion and emissive texture maps, bevels and GPU skinning.
- The CPU deforms morphs and skins once per pose.
- At most 4 lights cast shadows, and 8 shadow maps are drawn in all. A point light takes 6 of them.

### CSS

- `background-repeat: space` with thousands of gradient tiles still errors. The other repeats are drawn as one tile repeated by `feTile`.
- Backdrop blur and glow are capped at 128 px. The `blur` effect takes up to 512.

### GSAP

- A tween that plays by itself, without a `seek` on a paused timeline, does not advance. The render's clock is virtual and GSAP's ticker does not run between frames.
- `Flip`, `ScrollTrigger` and `Draggable` are not tested. `ScrollTrigger` has nothing to scroll in a render.
- An SVG shape's `getBoundingClientRect` is worked out from its `getBBox`, its transforms and the `<svg>`'s `viewBox`, not from layout. Strokes and filters are not in it.
- More in [GSAP](/docs/compat/#gsap).

### `@remotion/skia` and `@shopify/react-native-skia`

- `Atlas`, `Patch`, `Glyphs`, `TextBlob`, `Picture` and `ImageSVG` are drawn. Text inside an SVG is not.
- Not supported: Reanimated values, `FitBox`, `Skia.Surface` and `Skia.Offscreen`, the `Skia.Image`, `Skia.Data` and `Skia.Typeface` factories, `useVideo`, animated images, paragraph measurement, and shaders or filters set on a `Skia.Paint()` object.
- Drawing is on the CPU only.
- PNGs of drawn frames are kept under `~/.cache/kinetograph/skia`, the newest 400 to 600 of them.
- More in [AUTHORING.md](https://github.com/0xS4N4TG/kinetograph/blob/main/packages/cli/skills/kinetograph/references/AUTHORING.md), section 7.7.

### `@remotion/rive`

- Not supported: `onLoad`, `assetLoader`, `effects` and the `ref` getters.
- A state machine starts with its inputs at their defaults, and you can't set them.
- Only pictures embedded in the file are supported.
- More in [AUTHORING.md](https://github.com/0xS4N4TG/kinetograph/blob/main/packages/cli/skills/kinetograph/references/AUTHORING.md), section 7.6.

## Can look different

- **`preserve-3d` with planes that cross.** Groups with `transform-style: preserve-3d` under `perspective` are drawn back to front, with no depth buffer. Planes that cross each other are drawn wrong.
- **Inline-block with a stacking context is drawn twice.** A `display: inline-block` element that also has a `transform`, or any other property that makes it a stacking context or positions it, comes out as two identical layers. Its antialiased edges look slightly heavier, and it costs two draws. To reproduce it: a div with the text "ab", and a `span` with `display: inline-block; transform: scale(0.5)`. The end card of the launch film (frames 1386 to 1520) has this case.
- **`@paper-design/shaders` is untested.** The engine has the methods this library needs, but the library itself has not been run through it. Its WebGL2 calls, such as `getShaderPrecisionFormat`, are untested.
- **A rare difference with `--concurrency 2`.** In one render out of several, `--concurrency 2` produced a different video from the same project. Other runs at 2 workers matched, and runs at 1, 3, 4 and 6 workers matched. The cause is not known. It may be a rare race, or a fault in that one run. Renders are meant to be identical at any concurrency, so a difference is a bug.
