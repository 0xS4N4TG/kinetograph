title: Example prompts
description: Prompts for common videos, each with the engine features it relies on and what to check.
---

Copy a prompt, swap in your words and files, and keep the structure. Each one uses only features listed on [What works](/docs/compat/), but none was rendered for this page, so expect to adjust. Every prompt ends with a check, because the agent can't see the result until it draws a frame.

## A title card

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-create A 5 second 1920 by 1080 title card at 30 fps. The words "Quarterly review" in Inter 700, white, centred, on #14171f. The words write on over 20 frames, hold, and fade out in the last 10. Show a strip.
```

Uses `Text` with per-character styling, `interpolate` and a registered font. Check that the text fits and the fade ends on the last frame.

## A vertical explainer

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup A 15 second vertical video, 1080 by 1920 at 30 fps, three beats of 5 seconds each. Beat 1: "Step 1" and the line "Export your notes". Beat 2: "Step 2" and "Pick a folder". Beat 3: "Step 3" and "Done". Background #f6f0e4, text #1d1a16, accent #e0553a, Newsreader for the headings. Join the beats with 12 frame slides from the right. Keep the total at 15 seconds. Show a strip and run lint.
```

Uses `TransitionSeries`. The transitions overlap the beats, so the prompt asks the agent to hold the length.

## Captions over a voiceover

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup The narration is public/audio/vo.wav and its timings are in public/captions/vo.srt. Caption it in 3 to 5 word pages in the lower third, Inter 800, white on a 60% black bar, and light the spoken word in #ffd23f. Put the audio in the render. Run lint and audio analyze.
```

Uses `parseSrt`, `createTikTokStyleCaptions` and `Audio`. You supply the audio and the timings.

## A photo montage

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup A 20 second 1920 by 1080 montage of the five photos in public/images/trip/. Each shows for 4 seconds with a slow push-in from 100% to 108%. Cross-fade between them over 15 frames. Add fine film grain over everything and a warm light leak across the second cut. Music from public/audio/bed.mp3 at 35%, faded out over the last 2 seconds. Show a strip and run audio analyze.
```

Uses `Image`, `interpolate`, `TransitionSeries` with `fade` and an `Overlay`, the `grain` effect and `LightLeak`.

## A chart that builds

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup A 10 second 1920 by 1080 bar chart of this data: Q1 12, Q2 18, Q3 27, Q4 41. The bars grow one after another over 20 frames each, left to right, then the Q4 bar turns #e0553a and its value counts up. Axis labels in Inter 36 px. Check npx kinetograph add --list --type component first, and use a chart from the registry if one fits.
```

Uses native shapes and `Text`, or a registry component. Check the value labels at the last frame.

## A product turntable in 3D

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup A 6 second 1920 by 1080 shot of public/models/bottle.glb on a dark floor, turning once, with one key light that casts a shadow and a slow camera push-in. Background #0d0f14. Run doctor and tell me if the model uses anything the engine can't read.
```

Uses `Scene3D`, `Model`, a `DirectionalLight` with `castShadow` and `Camera3D`. `doctor` names an unreadable glTF feature.

## A loop for a social post

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup A 2 second 800 by 800 loop at 30 fps: a ring of 12 dots that turns once, so frame 0 and frame 60 match. Dots #e0553a on #14171f. Then render it as out/loop.gif with every second frame.
```

Uses `rotation` from the frame number, and `render` to `.gif` with `--every-nth-frame`. A GIF has no sound. Check frames 0 and 59 side by side, since the loop repeats after 59.

## A lower third with transparency

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-render Make a 4 second 1920 by 1080 lower third on a transparent background: a bar slides in from the left over 12 frames, the text "Mara Okafor" and below it "Founder" fade in, hold, then everything leaves. Render it as a ProRes 4444 .mov that keeps the transparency, and tell me the codec and pixel format you used.
```

A composition's background is transparent unless you set one. ProRes 4444 with `yuva444p10le` and VP9 with `yuva420p` keep alpha. See [Rendering](/docs/rendering/) and the [CLI reference](/docs/reference/cli/#render). Open the file in your editor to confirm the transparency, since a still can't show it.

## A video from a template

```txt title="Suggested prompt"
/kinetograph-markup Run npx kinetograph add --list --type template and tell me what is there. Install the one closest to a 20 second product launch, change its title and accent colour through its props, and render a draft.
```

Uses the registry and `--props`. See [Brand and templates](/docs/tips/brand-templates/).

## Make your own

Take the closest prompt and change three things: the length, the copy and the look. Keep the last sentence, the check. If a prompt asks for something the engine can't draw, `doctor` says so before you wait for a render.
