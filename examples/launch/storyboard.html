Motion rules

  • Entrances ease out over 6–10 frames; exits ease in over 4–6 frames.
  • Overshoot on objects only, never on type.
  • One move per beat; the VO word leads the eye.
  • Whips use the engine's motion blur plus 2–3 frames of speed lines.
