What each shot is made of

  • Real 3D: Poly Haven "Vintage Video Camera" (CC0, a scanned 8 mm cine camera) with a CC0 studio HDRI, through scene3d and gradientMap for the thermal look.
  • snapcn: logo-flicker, unmodified, flipping through 16 frames of our own renders.
  • inspire.mp4 scene: rebuilt as shot 7: the "Your ___" line, cards that arrive tinted and settle to full colour, collapse to dark, then the type-on.
  • Showcase: the pixel melt from shot 1 of examples/showcase, same code, rim recoloured.
  • Handwritten overlays: "FILM", "IMPACT", screen-blended. Transition: 16mm burn 01. Grunge: overlay_6 (paper), overlay_2 (dust).
  • Numbers: S04 render times (1.25 s vs 8.86 s, measured 2026-10-15); API coverage 168 / 170 public exports, measured today against remotion 4.0.536.
