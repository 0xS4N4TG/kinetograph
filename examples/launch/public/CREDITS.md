# Assets

- `models/vintage_video_camera/`: "Vintage Video Camera" by Jenelle van Heerden (model, photography) and James Ray Cock
  (textures), Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/vintage_video_camera
- `hdri/studio_small_08_1k.hdr`: "Studio Small 08", Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_08
- `pool/`, `stills/`: frames of Kinetograph's own renders (`examples/showcase`, `showcase-3d`, `rajz-cards`, the
  papercut ep13 cut).
- `fx/` (not committed): licensed overlay packs (handwritten words, 16mm burn, grunge), rebuilt at 1080p by
  `tools/assets.sh` from `~/Downloads`.
- `mg/horse.json`: pen strokes traced by `tools/zoetrope/trace.mjs` from Eadweard Muybridge, "The Horse in Motion"
  (1878), Library of Congress scan (item 97502309), public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Horse_in_Motion_high_res.tiff
- `mg/print.jpg`: Kobayashi Kiyochika, "Night Stalls at Asakusa" (1881), woodblock print, Honolulu Museum of Art,
  public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Night_Stalls_at_Asakusa_by_Kobayashi_Kiyochika,_1881,_woodblock_print,_Honolulu_Museum_of_Art.jpg
- `mg/fred.png`: a frame of "Fred Ott's Sneeze" (Edison, 1894), public domain, as in `footage/fred.mp4`.
